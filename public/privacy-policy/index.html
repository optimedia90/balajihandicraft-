Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Woodenara handles information when you browse our website, contact us or place an order.

Information collected

Depending on your use of the site, we may collect details you provide such as name, email, phone number, delivery address, order details and customer support messages, as well as basic browser and site-usage information.

How information is used

We use information to process and deliver orders, communicate about purchases, respond to enquiries, provide support, maintain security and improve the shopping experience.

Service providers

Information needed to fulfil your request may be shared with service providers involved in hosting, payment processing, delivery, analytics or customer communications. We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

Cookies and security

Essential browser storage may support cart, wishlist, currency and website functionality. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

For requests about access, correction or deletion, contact info@woodenara.com. Legal and order-record requirements may apply. Last updated: 31 October 2026.
