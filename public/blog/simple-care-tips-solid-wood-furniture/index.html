Simple Care Tips for Solid Wood Furniture

Keep furniture looking its best with gentle cleaning, sensible placement and regular care.

By Woodenara Editorial Team

Published: 31 October 2026 · Reviewed: 31 October 2026

The editorial team prepares furniture guides using Woodenara catalogue specifications, material details, product-care information and customer support feedback. For our review and correction process, see the Editorial Policy.

Guide

For everyday cleaning, use a soft dry or slightly damp cloth and wipe spills promptly. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive pads and excess water. Keep wooden furniture away from prolonged direct sunlight and damp areas. Use coasters and protective pads, lift items rather than dragging them, and follow care instructions supplied with the product.
